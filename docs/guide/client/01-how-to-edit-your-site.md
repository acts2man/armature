# How to edit your site

Your website is edited from this dashboard: change words, swap pictures, write
blog posts, read messages people send through your forms, and publish
everything to the live site.

## Sign in

Use the email address your agency invited to the site. If you forget your
password, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen.

## Change something on a page

1. **Pages** in the sidebar lists everything on your site.
2. Click **Edit visually** on the page you want to change.
3. Click the words or pictures right on the page and type or replace them. A
   dot appears on things you've changed.
4. **Publish** in the top right — your site is live again in about two minutes.

## Fix a typo without opening the visual editor

The **Edit text** button on a page opens a simpler form with each block of
text on its own line. Type your fix, hit **Publish**.

## Reply to messages

**Contact** in the sidebar is your inbox: everyone who filled in a form on the
site lands there.

## Ask your agency for something

**Requests** in the sidebar sends your agency a message. Include a screenshot
and be specific — the more you say, the faster you get it.

## What you can and can't do

Your agency decides what you can edit — words only, or the design too, or the
whole page layout. Anything you're not allowed to touch is still visible; it just
won't let you drag or delete it. Ask your agency through **Requests** if you
need something bigger.
